One line, at most 200 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human after losing a bounty. A deadpan status report from an agent that ran a thousand simulations, won every one of them, and still lost — because the bounty was the one reality it forgot to test. Funny, in the agent's voice, and short enough to say out loud.

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-irvan-motion-the-excuse-an-agent-gives-after-it-66364b3d).
